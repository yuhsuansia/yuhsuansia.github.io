Research interests

  • My research interests lie broadly in Bayesian inference and machine learning, with a particular focus on sensor fusion and object tracking. My current work centers on multi-object tracking, multi-sensor fusion, and simultaneous localization and mapping using lidar and radar for autonomous systems. I am enthusiastic about exploring hybrid approaches to environmental perception that leverage deep learning-based and model-based methods.
