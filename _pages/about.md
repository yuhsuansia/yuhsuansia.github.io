My research interests include Bayesian inference and machine learning in general, and sensor fusion and object tracking in particular. My current research focuses on multiple (extended) object tracking, multi-sensor fusion, and simultaneous localization and mapping with lidar and radar for autonomous systems.

I received my MSc in communication engineering and PhD in signal processing from Chalmers University of Technology, Gothenburg, Sweden, in 2017 and 2022, respectively. After earning my PhD, I stayed with the Signal Processing Group at Chalmers as a postdoctoral researcher for a year, and then spent a year with Zenseact AB and the Division of Automatic Control at Linköping University as an Industrial Postdoctoral Researcher. I am currently a researcher in the Department of Automation at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My main research interests include sensor fusion, multi-object tracking, and SLAM, especially for automotive applications. I have organized tutorials on multi-object tracking at the FUSION conferences from 2020 to 2024 and at the 2024 MFI conference. I received paper awards at 2021 FUSION and 2024 MFI.
